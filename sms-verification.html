How do I get started ?

Sign up on ibsmspool.com fund your wallet using Paystack - quick and secure

Which platforms can I verify with ibsmspool?

WhatsApp, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, Twitter/X, Google, PayPal, Stripe, and hundreds more. If it sends SMS, ibsmspool can receive it.

Can I get a refund if the number doesn't work?

Yes. If no SMS is received during your active window, you're entitled to a full wallet credit refund. Request within 24 hours for Virtual Numbers, 48 hours for Rentals.

How do I fund my wallet?

Wallet funding is processed in Naira (NGN) via Paystack. Bank transfer, card, and USSD are all supported.
